Answers to common questions, and how to reach us.

What is Rioplatense Spanish?

It’s the Spanish spoken around the Río de la Plata — in Buenos Aires and Montevideo. Its best-known features are vos instead of tú (vos tenés, vos sos), the “sh” sound for ll and y (calle sounds like “ca-she”), a melodic, Italian-influenced intonation and plenty of local words, some from lunfardo, the city’s slang.

People from any Spanish-speaking country will understand you. You’ll just sound like you learned it in Buenos Aires.

How long is a lesson?

About 15 minutes. Each lesson is a series of short, hands-on steps — choosing, matching, building sentences, listening, role-playing a conversation and, if you like, speaking — so you’re doing something every few seconds instead of reading long explanations.

I’m a complete beginner. Is it for me?

Yes. The path starts at A1 with greetings and survival phrases and continues through A2, B1 and B2 to C1. If you already speak some Spanish, it’s a quick way to switch to how porteños actually talk.

Do I need an account?

No. There’s no sign-up. Your progress is stored on your devices and synced through your own iCloud account. See the privacy policy for details.

Do I have to use the microphone?

No. Speaking exercises are optional and only use the microphone if you allow it. Your voice is not recorded or sent to us.
